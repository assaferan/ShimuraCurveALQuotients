## The extension (what stage3o.m computes, and the conventions it depends on)

Setting: D*N squarefree, weight 2.  The differentials of X_0^D(N) are the D-new part of
S_2(Gamma_0(DN)), which is the direct sum over M | N of the OLDCLASSES of the newforms g of level
exactly D*M.  Let T be the set of primes dividing N/M.  The oldclass of g at level N is spanned by
the 2^#T pullbacks

    g | w_S ,  S a subset of T,    (f|w)(z) := nrd(w) j(w,z)^-2 f(w z),

where w_S = prod_{p in S} w_p and w_p is an element of the level-N Eichler order O_N of reduced
norm p normalising O_N (the Atkin-Lehner element).  f|w is the pullback of the differential f dz
under z -> w z, so g|w_S is the pullback of g under the degeneracy map X_0^D(N) -> X_0^D(M),
[z] -> [w_S z]; S = {} is the natural projection and S = {p} the second degeneracy map at p
(the analogue of f(z) -> f(pz)).  Any two choices of w_p differ by a unit of O_N of norm 1 on the
right, up to sign, so g|w_p does not depend on the choice.

Action of the Atkin-Lehner group on the oldclass.
  * q | DM: w_q at level N is also an Atkin-Lehner element at level M (it lies in O_M and
    normalises it, the two orders agreeing locally at q), so it acts on every g|w_S by the
    quaternionic eigenvalue eps_q(g) of g on X_0^D(M): eps_q(g) = a_g(q) for q | D and
    eps_q(g) = -a_g(q) for q | M (the convention of stage3n.m / nold.m, which the genus counts
    there validate).
  * p in T: (g|w_S)|w_p = g|w_{S xor {p}} with NO scalar, because w_p^2 = p u with u a unit of
    O_N of norm 1 (checked exactly in the code: w_p^2/p lies in O_N), and the weight-2 slash
    operator is trivial on scalars and on O_N^1.
  So the oldclass is the regular representation of {+-1}^T twisted by eps, and its eigenvectors are

    v_chi = sum_S chi(S) g|w_S ,   chi a character of {+-1}^T,  chi(S) = prod_{p in S} chi(p),

  with w_m eigenvalue  prod_{q | m, q | DM} eps_q(g) * prod_{p | m, p in T} chi(p).

  The W-fixed differentials of X_0^D(N)/W are therefore: the W-fixed newforms of level D*N, plus
  one v_chi for every (g, chi) with that product equal to 1 for all m in W.  noldx.m counts these
  and the count equals the genus at all seventeen open quotients and at all 48 control candidates
  tried (including the two-prime classes T = {2,23} at X_0^15(46), T = {2,17} at X_0^21(34),
  T = {3,5} at X_0^26(15)).  That is the check of the eigenvalue bookkeeping.

Evaluation.  Theorem 3.1 at level M gives S_g(z1, z2) = y1 y2 conj(g_B(z1)) g_B(z2) from the
lattices R^(d), d | DM, of the level-M order O_M.  For the pullback, with j = j(w_S, z2),

    y1 y2 conj(g_B(z1)) (g_B|w_S)(z2)  =  (conj(j)/j) * S_g(z1, w_S z2),

because Im(w z) = nrd(w) y / |j|^2.  So each member of the oldclass is the level-M evaluator at
the MOVED second point, times a phase, with the SAME constant conj(g_B(z1)) in front as the
untransformed pullback; the column of v_chi is  sum_S chi(S) (conj(j_S)/j_S) S_g(z1, w_S z_j).
No normalisation is free here: the relative scale of the pullbacks is fixed by the slash operator,
and that is exactly what makes w_p act by +-1 on v_chi.  The newform columns are the level-N
evaluator unchanged (same algebra, same O_N = QuaternionOrder(A, N), same splitting, same points),
and the row factor y1 y_j is common to every column, so the matrix of values differs from the
one stage3n.m would build only by having the oldform columns added.

Orders and Atkin-Lehner elements.  O_1 := MaximalOrder(O_N); O_M := O_N + M O_1 (locally O_N at
the primes of M and O_1 elsewhere, so the Eichler order of level M containing O_N; discriminant
checked).  w_m is a short vector, for the Frobenius majorant of the real splitting, in the lattice
J_m = { x in O_N : trd(x y) in m Z for all y in O_N } -- the radical of O_N/mO_N, which is the
two-sided ideal of norm m (index m^2, checked) -- with nrd(x) = m exactly; such an x generates J_m
and hence normalises O_N (checked directly as well).  Magma's Normalizer is for definite orders
only, and its IsPrincipal can return a generator of negative norm (vwal.m), so neither is used.

Direct check of the sign and normalisation (CHECKW).  For each generator m of W the script
evaluates every column at w_m z as well and forms V(w_m z) conj(j)/j / V(z), which must be the
predicted eigenvalue (1 for the W-fixed columns).  The REJECTED characters of each oldclass are
carried along as extra columns so that their predicted eigenvalues (-1 under the generators that
flip them) are measured too.  A wrong relative scale c between g and g|w_p would show as c and 1/c
instead of +1 and -1.  For the newform columns this is a direct test of the Atkin-Lehner
convention, which before was tested only through dimension counts.

Where this would fail.
  * N not squarefree (p^2 | N): the oldclass has three pullbacks at p, w_p is not an involution on
    them, and J_m is no longer the radical description; the script refuses (require).
  * gcd(D, N) > 1 or D not squarefree: same refusal, and Theorem 3.1 is stated for Eichler orders.
  * weight k > 2 would need the phase (conj(j)/j)^(k/2) and the det^(k/2) slash; k = 2 only.
  * The Fourier coefficients are taken to the level-N bound nmax = DN (cut/4pi)^2 + 20 at every
    level, so the smaller levels are over-supplied, never under.
  * The conjugates of a newform orbit come from Magma as the same expansion over a number field
    with different embeddings; each embedding of Conjugates(K.1) is used once per orbit, which
    gives the right SET of forms whatever the matching, and the W-selection uses only the
    rational a_p at p | DM.
