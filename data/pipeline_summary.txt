WHAT THE ALGORITHM DOES
-----------------------
Goal: classify every Atkin-Lehner (AL) quotient X_0(D,N)/W of every Shimura
curve in range as SUB-HYPERELLIPTIC (geometric gonality <= 2, i.e. P^1, elliptic,
or hyperelliptic) or NOT.

Curves are never deleted from the working set; each is MARKED with a status:
  * open    -  IsSubhyp unassigned  (undetermined; could still be hyperelliptic)
  * ruled   -  IsSubhyp := false    (proven NOT sub-hyperelliptic)
  * proved  -  IsSubhyp := true     (positively classified P^1 / elliptic / hyperell.)

So "filtered out" at a step = how many curves newly became RULED OUT, and the
live candidate pool is the OPEN count. Filter* stages apply elimination criteria
(run in parallel); Update* stages are sequential bookkeeping that compute genera
and propagate each determination up and down the cover lattice.

The run proceeds in three phases:
  A. Star curves only      - the maximal AL quotient X* = X_0(D,N)/W* (one per (D,N)).
  Expansion                - each (D,N) is expanded into ALL 2^k AL quotients.
  B. All AL quotients       - per-curve filters on the full set.

NOTE on the new enumeration: the 772 pairs and 20645 quotients added by the
corrected bound contain exactly one hyperelliptic curve, X_0(94,9)/<w9,w94>

NOTE on special-fibre isomorphisms: a verdict is copied from X_0(D,N)/W' to
X_0(D,Np)/W only if the source is certified non-hyperelliptic at the same prime
p. Three D = 1 quotients got a wrong verdict this way in an earlier run before
that requirement: X_0(114)/<w2,w38>, X_0(130)/<w2,w26> and X_0(195)/<w5,w195>,
all hyperelliptic. They are now proved hyperelliptic by the canonical quadrics.

NOTE on the dual graph: 1172 curves are ruled out by the dual graph of the special
fibre at a prime p | D (519 star, 653 in Phase B). The stage covers levels N that
are not squarefree (the Atkin-Lehner ideal at a prime power is built from the
reflection of the lattice chain).

NOTE on attribution: the per-step "newly-ruled" deltas above count when a curve's
OPEN status first changed in the pipeline ordering. The attribution census below
counts curves by the TEST whose proof is recorded on the curve (TestInWhichProved).
Every filter that records a verdict guards with "if assigned IsSubhyp then continue"
(don't overwrite), so TestInWhichProved holds the FIRST test that determined each
curve -- the census and the stage-table deltas agree where a tag corresponds to a
single step (e.g. FilterByALFixedPointsOnQuotient: 14006 newly-ruled = 14006
recorded; FilterByComponentInvolution: 7757 = 7757; FilterByComplicatedALFixed-
PointsOnQuotient: 301 = 301; DualGraph: 519 + 653 = 1172). The two views still
differ where a tag aggregates work across phases or via lattice propagation rather
than a single step: the "Trace" tag (1956) collects both the Phase-A
FilterByTraceStar (1500) and the Phase-B FilterByTrace (456) rulings, and the
"HyperellipticALInvolution"/"GenusLt3"/closure tags are assigned in
UpdateByGenus/UpdateCurves and then propagated through the cover lattice. Treat
the stage table as the makespan ("when first determined") view and the census as
the first-writer "which test determined it" view.
