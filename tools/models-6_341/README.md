# The genus-four quotient X_0^6(341)/<w_3, w_11, w_62>: a model derived from the star

`Y = X_0^6(341)/<w_3, w_11, w_62>` has genus four and is a double cover of the genus-two star
`C = X_0^6(341)*`, branched at the images of the fixed points of `w_2` and `w_22` (the CM points of
discriminants −24 and −88, one rational point each). The star's equation is the one fitted to its
differentials (`star-model.log`, 58 good primes against the newform), a candidate; everything
below is exact relative to it. `tests/Hyperelliptic6_341.m` makes the branch pair canonical up to
the star's torsion, which the model shows is Z/2, generated by the difference of its two rational
Weierstrass points `W_1`, `W_2`; the remaining alternative, a cover branched at `W_1 + W_2`, would
need a rational square root of that torsion point, which does not exist (Magma's `IsDivisibleBy`),
so given the star the cover is hyperelliptic. Two rational Weierstrass points mean two rational
theta characteristics, hence two normal forms `y^2 = q1(t^2/c)` per fibre, with `u = (x - x0)/(x - e)`
and `e` either Weierstrass point; both are enumerated. In the coordinate `x -> 1/x` of the simplified
model the Weierstrass points are `x = 0` and `x = 3`. Run from the repository root:

| script | what it shows |
|---|---|
| `enumerate.m`, `enumerate_e3.m` | over every fibre of the star above a rational point of height ≤ 10^6 and every twist supported on {−1, 2, 3, 11, 31}, with the base `e = 0`, resp. `e = 3`: no match with `e = 0`; exactly one with `e = 3`, the fibre `x = 1/3` with `c = −3` |
| `thuemahler.m` | the branch fibre, exactly: the two branch points stay distinct modulo every prime of good reduction, so the fibre's weighted y-coordinate is a unit outside {2, 3, 11, 31}, and since the sextic is `x (x − 3) Q4(x)` with S-unit resultants, `Q4(a, b)` is an S-unit; Gherga–Siksek's solver gives all 56 solutions for both signs (`thuemahler.sols`, 389 s + 384 s), four fibres, and over both bases and 32 twists exactly one triple matches the counts |
| `verify.m` | the resulting curve, its two genus-two quotients (the star and `C'`), its counts against the trace formula at the 31 good primes below 150, and the conductor of `C'` |
