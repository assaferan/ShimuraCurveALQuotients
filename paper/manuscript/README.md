# The manuscript

`arXiv_v1.tex` and `arXiv_v1.bib` are the source of arXiv:2606.25153 (v1, June 2026), *Hyperelliptic
Atkin-Lehner quotients of Shimura curves*, E. Assaf and S. Hashimoto, copied here so that the notes under
`paper/` can refer to its sections and labels. The live version is edited on Overleaf; this copy is a
snapshot, not the place to edit. `data/pipeline_tables.tex` and `data/pipeline_figures.tex` are generated for
it by `make_latex_tables.m` and `make_latex_figures.m`.
